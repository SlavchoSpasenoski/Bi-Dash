GOOD TO KNOW

Clear answers from the start.

Is Bi-Dash really free?

Yes. It is available under the BSD-2-Clause license, with no license subscription. You provide infrastructure and maintenance. Paid AI providers charge their own fees.

Do I have to use AI?

No. SQL queries, charts and dashboards work without AI. For AI features, choose Ollama and download a model, or enter your own API key in Settings → AI Configuration. Review generated queries and results.

Where is my data stored?

The application and metadata live on your infrastructure. Bi-Dash queries your connected sources. If you choose an external AI provider, AI requests are sent to that provider under its policies.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes, with an appropriate server configuration. The local setup listens on 127.0.0.1:5001. For company access, configure HTTPS, networking, permissions and backups. The Linux guide describes private access through an SSH tunnel.

What if installation fails?

Check that Docker is running and Compose v2 is available. See the troubleshooting section in the guide. Report your operating system, architecture and error message without passwords or API keys.
