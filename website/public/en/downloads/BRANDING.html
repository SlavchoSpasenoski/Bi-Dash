Bi-Dash visual identity

The Rising Signal mark uses three ascending, faceted data segments and a separate yellow dash. It has no letter B, enclosing square or stock icon. The mark represents data becoming a clearer signal. The horizontal wordmark is used on account screens; the compact mark is used in navigation, loading screens, notifications, shared dashboards and browser tabs.

Product line: Your data. A clearer direction.

Editable SVG masters, a dark-background variant, a monochrome mark, PNG icons, an ICO and a preview are under branding/. Production vectors are under client/app/assets/images/. Keep the mark’s proportions, allow at least 8/64 of its width as clear space and use the light-blue variant on dark backgrounds. Minimum icon size: 16 px. Do not enclose the mark in a blue square. Semantic success, warning and error colors remain independent of the brand palette.
