---
title: Adding a Profile Picture
---

If you login to Bi-Dash with a Google account, Bi-Dash will automatically fetch your Google Account profile picture. Alternatively, Bi-Dash uses [Gravatar](https://en.gravatar.com/) to handle profile pictures.

If you don't have a Gravatar account, sign up for Gravatar with the email address you used to sign-in to Bi-Dash.

Gravatars are cached by your browser. Once signed up or updated, your Gravatar will appear on Bi-Dash within 5 minutes after the change following a page refresh.
